About SmartChat

SmartChat is a custom AI assistant built on top of the Qwen2.5 model and fine-tuned by Sahil to stay as neutral and safe as possible. Instead of blindly copying other chatbots, it is trained with a focused dataset that:

  • Avoids picking “best” religions, genders, races or countries.
  • Gives balanced, context-aware answers on sensitive topics.
  • Helps with C, C++, Python and other coding questions.
  • Supports study, productivity and relationship advice.

The goal is simple: an assistant that thinks with you, not for you. SmartChat explains trade-offs, offers perspectives and respects your values.

The model currently runs on Google Colab and can later be moved to a dedicated server or cloud GPU when needed.
